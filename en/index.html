Questions

What people usually ask me.

When do I pay?

When you see your preview and decide to go ahead. The preview comes before the invoice: by the time you pay, you have already seen your website. If you don't like it, you pay nothing.

What is the preview like?

I send you a link and you see it finished on your phone, just as your customers would see it. I hand over the files, the domain and the publication when you pay.

Is the website really mine?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the website is yours. If one day you want to move elsewhere, you take it with you.

Why don't you use WordPress?

Because your website doesn't need it: I build it with just the code it needs. It loads faster, it doesn't break, and there are no plugins to update.

Will I show up on Google?

Yes: I prepare it so Google understands it — clear texts, well-set titles and loading speed, which is what Google rewards.

How long does it all take?

Your preview, in 7 days. If you like it, your website goes live within 7 days of payment.

What if I want to change something later?

Message me on WhatsApp and we'll change it. Between €20 and €50/hour depending on what it is; if it's something bigger, I'll quote you first.

Another question? Message me on WhatsApp, I'll answer you myself.
